04 · Meta Ads Management

Strategic Facebook & Instagram Advertising Built Around Real Conversions

Meta Ads let you put your offer in front of ideal buyers across Facebook and Instagram before they even start searching. We plan, launch, and optimise paid social campaigns backed by accurate conversion tracking and disciplined creative testing.

Service overview

Turning paid social attention into measurable enquiries and sales

Facebook and Instagram offer remarkable reach, but boosting random posts or running a single generic ad rarely delivers sustainable results. Effective Meta Ads management combines three elements: reaching the right audience segments, presenting thumb-stopping creative that communicates a clear offer, and sending clicks to a fast landing page with reliable conversion tracking.

We structure Meta campaigns around your full buyer journey. Prospecting campaigns introduce your brand and offer to carefully defined interest, lookalike, or broad audiences; retargeting campaigns follow up with people who engaged with your social profiles or visited your website without converting.

Every campaign is built inside your own Meta Business Manager and Ad Account so you retain full ownership of your data, audiences, and history. We test creative angles methodically, monitor cost per result, and report in clear business terms.

What this service covers

  • Facebook & Instagram campaign strategy
  • Custom, lookalike & interest audience targeting
  • Ad creative planning & variation testing
  • Meta Pixel & conversion tracking setup
  • Retargeting campaigns & budget optimization

Problems we help solve

Common Meta advertising pitfalls we solve

Understanding what is holding performance back is the fastest way to decide what to fix first.

Boosted posts with no conversion goal

Spending budget on engagement boosts without proper campaign objectives or landing pages rarely produces qualified leads or sales.

Missing or broken Meta Pixel tracking

If conversion events are not firing accurately, Meta’s algorithm cannot optimise for buyers and you cannot see true campaign ROI.

Creative fatigue & rising costs

Showing the exact same graphic to the same audience for months causes click-through rates to drop and cost per result to climb.

No follow-up for warm website visitors

Most first-time visitors do not enquire immediately; without structured retargeting, that paid traffic is lost.

In-depth focus

How we structure high-performing Meta Ad accounts

Every campaign layer has a defined role, audience, and measurement metric:

Audience prospecting

Reaching new buyers across Facebook and Instagram Feeds, Stories, and Reels using data-backed audience signals and creative hooks.

Creative & format testing

Testing static visuals, carousels, and short-form video hooks to identify which messages earn the strongest response.

Multi-window retargeting

Re-engaging website visitors, video viewers, and social engagers with relevant proof, FAQs, and clear calls to action.

Pixel & event verification

Configuring and testing Meta Pixel and standard/custom conversion events so optimisation follows real enquiries or purchases.

Landing page alignment

Ensuring the page visitors land on matches the visual and headline promise of the ad they clicked.

Budget & bid discipline

Allocating spend toward winning ad sets and pausing underperforming variations before budget is wasted.

What is the difference between Meta Ads and Google Ads?

Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) reaches people based on interests, demographics, behaviours, and custom audiences while they browse social feeds—making it ideal for creating demand and retargeting. Google Ads reaches people actively typing search queries—making it ideal for capturing existing intent. Many businesses use both together.

Who pays Meta for the ad spend?

Your business pays ad spend directly to Meta via the billing method in your own Meta Ad Account. Our management fee is separate so you always have 100% visibility into exact media costs.

Do you guarantee a specific cost per lead or ROAS?

No honest agency can guarantee exact auction outcomes because results depend on market competition, your offer, pricing, and landing page conversion rate. We commit to accurate tracking, structured creative testing, and disciplined budget management.

Can you help if we do not have a landing page yet?

Yes. Through our Website Development and Lead Generation services, we can design and build a dedicated conversion landing page before launching your Meta campaigns.
