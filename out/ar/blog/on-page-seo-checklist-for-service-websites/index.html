A Practical Search Engine Optimization Checklist for Service Websites

Learn how search intent, page structure, title tags, headings, internal links, and image alt text help both search engines and potential clients understand your service pages.

Published February 24, 20256 min read

On this page

  2. 2. Write clear title tags, meta descriptions, and headings
  3. 3. Connect related pages with descriptive internal links
  4. 4. Add accurate structured data where appropriate

1. Match one clear topic and search intent per page

When a single page tries to rank for six distinct services, it usually struggles to explain any of them well. Give each core service its own dedicated page so the title, headings, examples, and FAQs can focus completely on what that buyer needs to know.

Before writing or editing a page, look at what types of pages currently appear for that topic. Are searchers looking for a service provider, a step-by-step guide, or a comparison? Align the depth and format of your page accordingly.

2. Write clear title tags, meta descriptions, and headings

Your title tag and meta description are often the first impression someone gets of your business in search results. Once they click through, your heading hierarchy (a single H1 supported by logical H2 and H3 subheadings) helps them scan the page in seconds.

  • Use one descriptive H1 that states what the page is about in plain language.
  • Organise key buyer questions into H2 sections: what the service includes, problems solved, process, and FAQs.
  • Write unique title tags and meta descriptions for every indexable page—avoid duplicating homepage metadata across inner pages.

3. Connect related pages with descriptive internal links

Internal links help visitors navigate to related services and help search engines understand how your pages relate to one another. Instead of generic "click here" links, use natural anchor text that describes the destination page, such as linking from a website development guide to your Search Engine Optimization service.

Pair clean internal linking with optimised images: use modern formats like WebP or SVG, specify width and height attributes to prevent layout shifts, and write concise alt text that describes informative visuals for screen readers and search engines.

4. Add accurate structured data where appropriate

JSON-LD structured data—such as Organization, Service, BreadcrumbList, and BlogPosting markup—helps search engines parse the entities on a page. Keep structured data strictly factual and consistent with what is visible to visitors on the page, and remember that schema markup supports understanding rather than guaranteeing rich results or rankings.
